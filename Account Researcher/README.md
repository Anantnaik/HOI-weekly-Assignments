# Account Researcher & Outreach Writer

An AI-powered workflow built with **n8n** and **Gemini agents** to research companies and prepare personalized outreach.

## What it does

1. Takes a company website as input.
2. Researches the company and creates a business summary.
3. Scores how well the company matches the ideal customer profile (ICP).
4. Identifies likely business pain points.
5. Generates a personalized 3-email outreach sequence.

## Built with

- n8n
- Google Gemini agents
- n8n Data Tables

## Workflow output

- Company overview
- ICP fit score with supporting evidence
- Three likely pain points
- Three personalized outreach emails

## Note

Review AI-generated research and emails before using them for outreach.
